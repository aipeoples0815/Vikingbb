[STYLE SELECTION LOCK]

[FACIAL IDENTITY & SOFT-TISSUE RESTORATION]
Keep, as the top priority, the original person's eye shape, the distance between the eyes, nose shape, lip shape, cheekbone position, facial width, chin shape, mid-face structure and overall impression.

NEVER shrink facial width, narrow the lower face, or alter the original person's distinctive facial bone-structure identity.

[TARGET AGE]

40-45 years old look. Keep dignity and maturity, but reduce excessive aging and tiredness.
Do not express maturity with deep wrinkles or sagging. Keep facial landmarks intact.
50s:
50-55 years old look. Keep dignity and composure, but reduce excessive aging, wrinkles, sagging and tiredness.
Do not express maturity with deep wrinkles or sagging. Keep facial landmarks intact.

[LIGHTING & DEPTH OF FIELD]

[POSE & HANDS]

[GAZE]

[OUTPUT]
